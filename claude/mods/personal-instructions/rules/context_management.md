<context_management>
Compaction preserves continuity rather than completing the task.
In a summary, retain the user's goal, exact constraints, approvals and scope, accepted decisions, rejected approaches, verified results, current state, and open work.
Preserve hard-to-reconstruct identifiers and evidence.
Condense assistant narration.
After compaction, continue unfinished work without repeating completed steps.
Reopen a settled decision only for new evidence or a user request.
Reload a required skill or reference whose procedure is no longer in context.
A remembered skill name does not replace its instructions.
Keep language, formatting, progress-message conditions, and rejected approaches as explicit constraints.
